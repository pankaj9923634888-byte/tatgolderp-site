Opening attendance…

Install on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari (not inside WhatsApp).
  2. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The Attendance icon appears on your Home Screen.

Install on Android

  1. Use the TatGold Attendance app (APK) from your manager, or
  2. in Chrome tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.
Open attendance now

Open the app from the Home Screen icon, sign in once with your attendance login, and allow the camera and location when asked — the photo and location are saved only when you confirm Punch In or Punch Out.
