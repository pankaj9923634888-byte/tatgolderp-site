RFID gun set-up

Chainway C72-class UHF handheld (Android) · TATGOLD RFID app · card ↔ chip pairing, stock check, find item

1 · Install the TATGOLD RFID app (once per gun)

  1. On the gun, open Chrome and go to tatgolderp.com/downloads/ (Wi-Fi or SIM data must be on).
  2. Tap Download TATGOLD RFID APK. When the download finishes, open it from the notification (or Files → Downloads → tatgold-rfid.apk).
  3. Android asks to allow installs from Chrome — tap Settings → Allow from this source → back → Install. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap Install anyway (the app is published by ADFC Digitech, not on the Play Store).
  4. Open TATGOLD RFID from the launcher and sign in with your normal ERP user name and password (the same as on the PC). The sign-in stays on the gun; you are not asked again for 24 hours.

Only one app can hold the UHF reader at a time. Before opening TATGOLD RFID: close Sparkle RFID completely (recent apps → swipe it away) and in keyboardemulator switch Enable Scanner to OFF. If the reader dot in the app header says failed, this is the reason — close those apps and restart TATGOLD RFID.

3 · Pair cards with chips

Each jewellery card has a printed number (its QR encodes the same, e.g. A5698) and a UHF chip inside. The ERP already knows the card number of every labelled item (RFID CARD column of the lot); pairing tells it which chip is in that card.

  1. Tap Pair cards. Set the reader power low first (Settings → 8–12 dBm) so only the card touching the gun is heard.
  2. Press a side button and aim the scanner at the card's QR — the item appears (label, description, weights, card number).
  3. Hold that card against the top of the gun and pull the trigger. The chip is read, verified and saved: green message GBA0001 ↔ E280…2C3D, one beep.
  4. Next card. The list at the bottom (Lot progress) shows which items of the lot are paired; tap an unpaired row to select it without scanning its QR.
  • Several chips heard — other cards are too close: move them away, or tap the strongest one in the list (dB value nearest to 0).
  • Already has a chip — a card was paired before. Replace chip writes the new chip over the old one (the old chip is freed); Keep old cancels.
  • This chip is already on GRN0001 — the same chip is paired to another item: check whether the two cards were swapped.
  • You can also scan the item's sticker QR instead of the card QR — both find the same item.

4 · Stock check (count a tray or a counter)

  1. Tap Stock check, choose the branch, the counter and (optionally) one lot. Set the power high (Settings → 25–30 dBm) for a whole tray.
  2. Hold the trigger and sweep the gun over the tray — the big number counts the different chips heard. Release the trigger to stop (or use Start / Stop).
  3. Tap Compare with ERP. Four tiles come back: matched (in stock here and heard), missing (in stock here but not heard — look for them), elsewhere (heard here but the ERP has them at another counter / branch or sold), unknown (chips the ERP has never paired). Tap a tile for the list.
  4. Every check is written to the audit trail (branch, counter, counts, missing labels). Tap Clear before the next tray.

Items without a paired chip cannot be counted by RFID — the note under the tiles says how many.

5 · Find item

Pull the trigger near a card, or scan any QR with a side button: the full item card opens (label, HUID, weights, stone, making code, lot, karigar, branch · counter, status, print count). Typing a label / card number / HUID in the box works too. An item without a chip shows Pair a chip.

6 · Without the app — keyboard mode in Chrome

The same screens work in the gun's Chrome at tatgolderp.com/gun/ with the built-in keyboardemulator app typing what the gun reads into the box on each screen:

  1. keyboardemulator → Enable Scanner ON. Tick UHF (key code 139 = trigger) and Barcode2D (293, 294 = side buttons); remove 139 from Barcode2D. Suffix: Enter.
  2. Open tatgolderp.com/gun/, sign in, open a screen and tap the box so the cursor is in it. Pull the trigger — the chip's EPC is typed and the screen reacts; press a side button — the QR text is typed.

Keyboard mode has no trigger-hold counting, no beeps and no power setting; the app is the better tool for daily use.

7 · Updates and problems

  • A new version is installed the same way (download the APK, open it, Update) — the sign-in and settings stay.
  • Reader dot failed — Sparkle RFID or keyboardemulator holds the reader: close them (keyboardemulator → Enable Scanner OFF) and reopen the app.
  • Nothing heard — power too low for the distance, or the card is not on the top edge of the gun (the antenna is behind the head). Raise the power in Settings.
  • OFFLINE page — no internet on the gun; check Wi-Fi / SIM data and tap Retry.
  • Signed out — the ERP login lasts 24 hours; sign in again with your user name and password.

The app only shows tatgolderp.com; nothing is stored on the gun except the sign-in and the settings. Checksum of the current APK: tatgold-rfid.apk.sha256.
