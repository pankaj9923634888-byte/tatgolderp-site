TATGOLD RFID

For the Chainway UHF gun: pair jewellery cards with their chips, count a tray against the ERP stock, find an item by chip or QR. Uses your normal ERP login.

Download Android APK · 1.1
  1. Open this page in the gun's Chrome and download the APK.
  2. Open the file and allow installation from Chrome if Android asks.
  3. Close Sparkle RFID and switch keyboardemulator's Enable Scanner OFF, then open TATGOLD RFID and sign in.
  4. Pair cards with the side button (QR) and the trigger (chip).

Android 7 or later · Chainway C72-class handheld with the UHF module · Internet required. Set-up guide and buttons · checksum

TatGold Attendance

Use your TatGold login to mark attendance with a photo, time and current location.

Download Android APK · 1.0
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and download the APK.
  2. Open the file and allow installation from your browser if Android asks.
  3. Sign in, open HR & Attendance and allow Camera and Location.
  4. Take your photo, review it and confirm Punch In or Punch Out.
